# Product lenses — load only when triggered

Use one or two lenses when they change a decision. Never require every framework.

| Trigger | Lens | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Users describe goals/workarounds but requested features are unclear | Jobs to Be Done | Situation, progress sought, forces of switching, competing workaround |
| Many ideas compete before solution selection | Opportunity Solution Tree | Connect outcome → evidenced opportunities → solution options → experiments |
| Stakeholder value conflicts or monetization is unclear | Value proposition / desirability-feasibility-viability | Who gains what, at what cost, and whether delivery/business constraints hold |
| Frontstage and operations do not close | Service blueprint | User action → visible service → backstage process → support system/evidence |
| Priorities compete | RICE or cost-of-delay, only with credible inputs | Make assumptions visible; do not convert invented scores into truth |
| Success metrics risk gaming | Outcome + guardrail metrics | Pair desired behavior with quality, safety, fairness, cost, and abuse limits |
| A proposed constraint may be inherited convention | First-principles decomposition | Separate objective, physical/technical facts, policy choices, and conventions |
| Harm, exclusion, trust, or AI behavior matters | Responsible-product pre-mortem | Affected parties, misuse/failure, detection, mitigation, appeal, shutdown |

Document the selected lens, why it applies, what decision changed, and what evidence is still missing. If no lens changes a decision, mark `NOT_APPLICABLE` and continue.
